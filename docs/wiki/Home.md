# 🧘 Welcome to the Zen TV Wiki

**Zen TV** is a high-performance, distraction-free, ultra-lightweight launcher built with **Jetpack Compose for TV (Material 3)**.

It is specifically engineered for resource-constrained Smart TVs and TV sticks (such as Xiaomi Mi TV 4A 32", Fire TV Stick, Google TV / Chromecast, and Android TV boxes).
